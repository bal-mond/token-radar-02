How to read this radar. A protocol “records no token” when the snapshot carries no market cap, no gecko id and no symbol for it — that is DefiLlama's record, not proof it has none. Implied value = fitted curve value × fitted token odds. The P10–P90 band is the 10th–90th percentile of the leave-one-out residuals. Farm plans are costed in three deposit tiers (small / standard / large) with chain-based gas; the verdict comes from the standard tier at an assumed 0.005% farmer share. Traded rows are called on their curve multiple instead: below 0.70× FARM, 0.70–1.50× THIN, above 1.50× SKIP.

Method, in one paragraph. The size curve log(mcap) = a + b·log(TVL) is least squares over the snapshot's own 128 traded tokens with a recorded market cap. Token odds are a logistic regression P(listed | age) fitted on the snapshot's own 645 known-age protocols, turned into ever-lists odds by the survival identity with pi from the oldest decile. Every extra term — quadratic, per-category curves, chain count, growth, age, Huber, trimmed — was fitted and tested held-out; the Model tab prints the numbers and why the plain curve ships. The program runs twice and writes identical bytes; it reads only the sealed snapshot and reaches no network.
